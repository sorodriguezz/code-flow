Double ping (`notify`), Ping (`ping`) and Soft bell (`halo`) are generated by Web
Audio in `src/lib/notificationSound.ts`, matching the catalogue's preview pitches,
timings, attack/decay envelopes and dynamics processing. The recordings' playback
gains follow TypeUI's RMS normalization. All sounds use CodeFlow's volume slider.

Prisma, the tenth cue, is an original CodeFlow composition: three rising plucked
tones (E5, A5, D6), a quiet chord under the last note and a short echo. It is
generated with Web Audio and covered by CodeFlow's own licence.
